BGMI Guide

Battle royale fundamentals to help you survive longer and win more fights.

BGMI
  1. Choose your landing wisely. Land away from crowded hot drops while you learn, loot a gun, armor and a helmet fast.
  2. Play with headphones. Footsteps and gunshots tell you where enemies are before you see them.
  3. Fight from cover. Peek and lean around walls instead of standing in the open.
  4. Control recoil. Use short bursts at range and pull down slightly while spraying.
  5. Rotate early. Move toward the safe zone before the circle shrinks so you avoid last-minute fights.
  6. Carry utility. Smoke grenades and frags help you revive teammates and push or escape safely.

Free Fire MAX Guide

Fast-paced match tips for quicker rounds and sharper fights.

Free Fire MAX
  1. Agree on a drop spot. Land together with your squad so nobody fights alone.
  2. Pick a character that fits your role. Choose abilities that match how you play, like healing, movement or support.
  3. Use gloo walls. Place them for instant cover while you heal, reload or push.
  4. Keep your crosshair at head level. Good crosshair placement saves time and wins close duels.
  5. Watch the zone and your health. Heal in cover and do not push with low HP.
  6. Practice in the Training Ground. Test weapons and your sensitivity before ranked games.

How to Improve Your Gameplay

Habits that make a real difference over weeks, not just one match.

Aim and Sensitivity

Start with the generator, then fine-tune in the Training Ground. Change a little at a time and give each setting a few days.

Optimize Your HUD

Try a 3 or 4 finger claw layout. Resize buttons so you can aim, move and shoot without lifting your thumbs.

Learn the Map

Know good landing spots, cover, and safe rotation routes. Map knowledge beats raw aim.

Play as a Team

Share callouts like enemy direction and health. Stay close enough to revive or trade kills.

Review Your Games

Record matches and watch your deaths. Look for repeated mistakes, then fix one at a time.

Take Care of Yourself

Take breaks, drink water and sleep well. Tired players make slower decisions.

Tips & Tricks

Quick wins you can use in your next match.

Smoother Performance

  • Lower graphics if you see lag or heat
  • Close background apps before playing
  • Keep some storage free
  • Avoid charging while gaming to limit heat

Better Fights

  • Hold angles instead of chasing
  • Reload behind cover
  • Use sound to time your push
  • Take fights you can win

Smarter Looting

  • Take one main weapon you like
  • Keep heals and grenades ready
  • Do not over-loot while the zone closes

Late Game

  • Hold a safe edge position
  • Let others fight first
  • Stay patient and avoid open ground
